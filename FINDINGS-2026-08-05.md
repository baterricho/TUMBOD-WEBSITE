The roster and the two hotlines are done. What remains still needs the barangay,
but the ask is now much smaller — and, more usefully, it is now a *correction*
task rather than a *compilation* task. Confirming a draft is far less work than
producing one, so it is far more likely to come back.

Send the Secretary (**Julie B. Batiancela**) the live Officials page and ask:

1. **Is any name or spelling wrong?** It is published — errors are now visible.
2. **Which committee does each Kagawad chair?** Seven answers, one line each.
3. **Who are the 7 SK Kagawad?**
4. **Photo consent** — per person, yes or no. This is the biggest remaining gap
   in how alive the site feels (`PRECEDENT-SCAN.md` §3 ranks it #1). Nothing is
   published without it.
5. **The other five hotlines** — BDRRMC, Coast Guard, RHU, MDRRMO, PNP. These
   are what a resident reaches for when the hall does not answer.
6. **Does a Barangay Tumbod Facebook page exist**, and what is its exact URL?
   Search found only the municipal page posting *about* Tumbod. If there is no
   barangay page, B1 and `FACEBOOK-EXTRACTION-WORKSHEET.md` are moot.

Anything that comes back enters the codebase with `verifiedAt` and `verifiedBy`
set, as `public/admin/config.yml` has always required and `types.ts` now does too.
